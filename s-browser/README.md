## New: CG City News
Open **News** from the bottom navigation or homepage (also accessible in the menu and Jobs hub).

- **City coverage:** All 33 Chhattisgarh district names, dozens of large cities and towns including Raipur, Bhilai, Durg, Rajnandgaon, Bilaspur, Dhamtari, Mahasamund, Saraipali, Raigarh, Korba, Ambikapur and Jagdalpur. You can enter **any other city, village or town by name** rather than relying solely on a predefined list.
- **Current on-demand feeds:** Google News RSS headlines are searched for the selected city and Chhattisgarh. Select Hindi or English; last 24 hours, 3 days or 7 days. Headlines outside the selected publication window are excluded, duplicates are reduced, and results are sorted latest first.
- **Transparent sourcing:** Every headline shows its publisher and the `pubDate` supplied by the feed, plus a link to read the original story through the news feed. The app shows when the feed was actually fetched. It does not invent news or mark a published headline as independently verified.
- **Safety and accuracy:** A news article can be outdated, incomplete, corrected or inaccurate even if freshly published. Check details on the publisher's original page before relying on it; no claims of 100% accuracy. If no recent items or service is offline, the app says so rather than silently substituting older stories.
- **Government updates:** Direct shortcut to the Chhattisgarh Directorate of Public Relations (DPR) news portal `https://jansampark.cg.gov.in/dprnewsportal/`, separate from third-party news.
- **Refresh:** On-demand refresh, no background news spying, no GPS permission, no accounts. The RSS provider is a third-party service whose availability and usage terms may change; this beta is intended for personal reading.

### Limits
This release does **not** claim exhaustive reporting for every Chhattisgarh locality. A user-entered place can be searched, but some places have no recent online coverage. News results and timestamps originate with publishers/the feed, not original fact-checking by S Browser. This is not a verified newsroom or licensed news syndication service. Google News RSS may limit the app's permitted uses; confirm provider terms before commercial deployment.

## Existing Jobs & Exams
City-based official directory for CG Vyapam, CGPSC, RRB Bilaspur, district recruitment notices, JEE, Navodaya and Sainik School. Its separate opt-in alert monitors official portal changes only; **a changed page does not mean a new confirmed vacancy**.

## Other Browser Tools
Native WebView, search, tabs, downloads, bookmarks, history, Study Focus, local notes, reading mode and **304 offline English–Hindi dictionary terms** with example information. App name **S Browser** and original S logo remain unchanged.

## Build & APK
Android Studio: open `s-browser/`, use JDK 17, Gradle 8.9 and Android SDK 35. GitHub Actions builds and verifies the debug-signed APK plus source/freshness tests. Latest release: [S Browser v1.3 Beta](https://github.com/saurabhsahu20-svg/Saurabhs-Vision-/releases/tag/s-browser-v1.3.0-beta).

A green CI build is not proof of real-phone performance or of source accuracy. Test browsing, article redirects and city search on your device. The source directory is public and the beta remains experimental.
